# School Agreement — Roster-Sharing and Fee-Ledger Clauses

**DRAFT — for counsel review.** Not yet signed. Version 0.1, [date].

Two clauses to be inserted into the agreement between **Inventre** ([legal entity name]) and each partner school ([School name], the "School"). Clause A applies to every school. Clause B applies **only** to schools whose fee ledger Inventre reads from MyClassBoard. Terms in bold have the meaning given in the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 (the "Act") and the DPDP Rules 2025 (the "Rules").

---

## Clause A — Student roster sharing (all schools)

**A.1 What the School shares.** To run the school-kit programme (uniforms, books, kits and related supplies ordered by parents on inventre.in), the School provides Inventre with its student roster, by file or by system feed, containing for each student: name; class, section and grade; enrolment number; house colour, medium and curriculum where relevant to the kit; and the name, mobile number(s) and e-mail of each parent or guardian who may act for the child. The School does not send any other field (for example date of birth, blood group, photographs or health information) unless the parties agree in writing that it is needed for the programme. [Delete or adjust the extra fields for schools whose feed already includes them.]

**A.2 The School's confirmation of consent.** The School confirms that it has obtained, and will maintain, the parents' or guardians' consent, in the manner the Act requires for a child's data, to share the roster with Inventre for the purposes in A.3, and that its own notice to parents refers to Inventre as the kit-programme supplier. On request the School provides Inventre with a copy of the wording it used. Where a parent withdraws consent or objects, the School informs Inventre within [7] working days and Inventre stops processing that child's data except where the law requires retention.

**A.3 Inventre's purposes.** Inventre, as **Data Fiduciary** for the storefront, uses the roster only to: (a) identify the parent by the mobile number the School holds and let them log in with a one-time password; (b) show each child the catalogue for their school and grade; (c) size, attribute, fulfil, deliver and exchange orders; (d) handle missing-item claims, exchanges and concerns; and (e) keep the records the tax laws require. Inventre does not use the roster for marketing unrelated to the programme, does not sell or share it for advertising, and does not track, behaviourally monitor or target children (section 9 of the Act). Inventre's processors (fulfilment ERP, payment gateway, SMS gateway, e-mail and backup provider, media storage) are listed in its privacy notice at inventre.in/privacy and engaged under written terms.

**A.4 Retention.** Inventre keeps a student's record while the child is on the School's roster and for **one year after** the roster shows the child has left, for exchanges and fee follow-up, then anonymises the child's name and identifiers. Order and invoice records are kept for the period tax law requires (8 years from the financial-year end) with the child's identity anonymised after the one-year period. Parent accounts with no active child and no order for 3 years are anonymised.

**A.5 Parents' rights.** Parents may exercise their rights under the Act (access, correction, erasure, grievance, nomination) either through the School, which forwards the request to Inventre within [3] working days, or directly through Inventre's grievance officer named at inventre.in/privacy ([grievance officer name], [e-mail]). Inventre responds within the period the Rules set (target 7 working days). Corrections to school, class or enrolment are made by the School in its roster and flow to Inventre; corrections to contact details may be made by the parent in their account.

**A.6 Security and breach.** Each party protects the roster with reasonable security safeguards. Inventre informs the School without undue delay, and in any case within [72] hours, of any personal-data breach affecting the School's students, before it notifies affected parents, and cooperates with the School on the parent communication. The School informs Inventre at it@inventre.in within [72] hours of any breach on its side affecting data it has shared with Inventre.

## Clause B — Fee-ledger processing (MyClassBoard schools only)

**B.1 Roles.** For the fee ledger, receipts and receivables data held in the School's MyClassBoard account, the **School is the Data Fiduciary** and **Inventre is a Data Processor** under section 8(2) of the Act. The School decides the purpose; Inventre processes only on the School's documented instructions, which are set out in this clause and may be varied by the School in writing.

**B.2 What Inventre does.** Inventre reads from MyClassBoard, using credentials the School authorises, the roster of fee-paying students (name, enrolment, class, section, status, guardian contact) and per student the fee heads billed, amounts due and paid, concessions, and receipt numbers and dates. Inventre imports this **nightly** and displays it in a fee console showing outstanding and collected fees, ageing and receipts, for the School's own use. Inventre writes nothing back to MyClassBoard.

**B.3 Who may see it.** Access is restricted to **fee-desk accounts** only: named users the School nominates in writing, plus [number] named Inventre staff who support the console. Fee-desk accounts use a separate login and cannot reach the storefront administration console. Inventre keeps a list of fee-desk users and provides it to the School on request; the School tells Inventre within one working day when a nominated user should lose access.

**B.4 No other use.** Inventre uses the fee data only to provide the fee console to the School. It does not combine the fee data with storefront order data, does not use it to decide what a parent may buy, does not contact parents about fees unless the School instructs it in writing, and does not disclose it to any third party other than its hosting and backup processors named in Clause A.3.

**B.5 Retention.** Inventre keeps the fee data for the **current academic year plus the three preceding academic years**, and deletes older rows. Backups holding deleted rows age out within [12] months. On termination of this clause, or earlier on the School's instruction, Inventre deletes the School's fee data within [30] days and confirms in writing; the School's MyClassBoard account remains the record of truth throughout.

**B.6 Security, breach and assistance.** Inventre applies the safeguards in Clause A.6 to the fee data, notifies the School at [School contact e-mail] within **24 hours** of becoming aware of any breach affecting it (so the School can meet its own notification duties under the Rules), assists the School with parents' requests concerning the fee data, and allows the School to verify compliance on reasonable notice no more than once a year. Inventre engages no sub-processor for the fee data other than those in Clause A.3 without prior written notice to the School.
