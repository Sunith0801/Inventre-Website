INVENTRE.Section A · First-time login

Section A — First-time login

Use this when the school has just added your child and you've never signed in before.

1Open the website.

Visit inventre.online on your phone or laptop and tap Sign in. You'll land on the screen on the right — a single field for your mobile number.

2Type your 10-digit mobile number.

No spaces, no +91 — just the 10 digits. Then tap Continue.

The website auto-removes spaces, dashes and the +91 prefix, but the underlying number must be exactly 10 digits.

3Enter your password (or the OTP, for first-time users).

If you're signing in for the very first time you'll be asked for a 6-digit OTP that arrives on your mobile. Then you'll set a password.

If you already have a password, just enter it and tap Sign in.

Tip: Once you've set your password, future logins only need the mobile + password — no OTP — unless you reset.
INVENTRE.Section B · Change your mobile (logged in)

Section B — Change your mobile (already logged in)

Use this when you can sign in with your current number and just want to switch to a new one.

1Sign in as usual at inventre.online.

2Tap your profile icon at the top right and choose My account.

You'll land on the dashboard shown on the right — your current mobile number is masked with the Change button next to it.

3Type your new 10-digit mobile.

Tap Send OTP. A 6-digit code will arrive on the new number within a minute.

We send the OTP to the new number so we know you really have access to it before the switch.

4Enter the 6-digit OTP that arrived on the new number.

Tap Verify. You'll see a confirmation screen and from your next login you'll use the new mobile.

Tip: Your old number stays in the system as a backup guardian contact until you ask the school admin to remove it. This is helpful if you ever need to recover access.
INVENTRE.Section C · Recover / add a mobile

Section C — "I don't remember which mobile is on file"

Use this when you can't sign in because the registered mobile is wrong, changed, or unknown.

1Open inventre.online and tap Sign in.

2At the bottom of the login screen, tap "Recover, change or add a mobile via student details".

3Pick your child's school from the first dropdown.

4Pick their grade from the second.

If you don't see your grade, double-check the school's spelling — some appear under variants (e.g. "St. Michaels" vs "St. Michaels School").

5Type at least 3 letters of your child's name.

Spelling doesn't have to be perfect — the search forgives typos, accepts words in any order, and you can even type the last 4 digits of the registered mobile number instead of the name.

6Tap your child's row. We mask the mobile on file with only the last 4 digits visible — enough to confirm it's the right account without exposing the full number.

7Verify it's you. Pick one of two methods:

  • OTP to the old mobile — use this if you still have access to the registered number.
  • OTP to your registered email — use this if the mobile is gone.

8Pick what to do next:

  • Change my number — replaces the old one (use this for a new SIM).
  • Add as extra guardian — keeps the old number and adds yours as a second contact.

9Enter the new 10-digit mobile, verify the OTP, and you're done.

INVENTRE.Section D · Troubleshooting

Section D — Troubleshooting

OTP didn't arrive

  • Wait a full minute — SMS delivery sometimes lags.
  • Check that you typed the mobile correctly (10 digits, no spaces, no +91).
  • Make sure your phone has signal and SMS isn't blocked by a DND setting on your mobile network.
  • Tap Resend OTP. The timer resets after 60 seconds.
  • For email OTP, also check the spam folder.

"No matching students found" when I search by name

  • Type just the first 3 letters of the first name (e.g. sun for Sunith).
  • Try the last 4 digits of the registered mobile instead — it's the most reliable.
  • Double-check the school and grade — children appear under one exact grade only.

I see the wrong child / wrong mobile in the search results

  • Stop. Don't proceed with the recovery — you'd change the wrong family's number.
  • Contact your school admin and they'll fix the mapping.

"Mobile number must contain 10 digits"

  • The site removes spaces, dashes and +91 from what you type, but the underlying number must be exactly 10 digits.
  • "+91 98765-43210" is fine. "98765-43" is not — it's only 7 digits.
Still stuck? Reach out to your school's Inventre coordinator with: your child's name, school, grade, and the mobile number you'd like on file. They can update it for you directly.
